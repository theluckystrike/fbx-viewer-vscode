# Free Zovo extensions for the files next to your models

- STL Viewer, OBJ, PLY, GLB & glTF Preview opens the files you convert to.
- TGA Viewer & DDS, KTX Textures opens the textures of your models.
- USD Viewer opens USDA, USDC and USDZ scenes.

Each link opens the extension page. Nothing installs until you click Install there.
