# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release. Opens FBX (binary and ASCII, version 7.0 and later), Collada .dae and 3D Studio .3ds models in a 3D view drawn with three.js.
- Orbit, pan, zoom and Fit; grid, axes, wireframe, vertex normals, bounding box and skeleton toggles; theme, dark or light background; Y or Z up.
- Animation clips: clip list, play and pause, timeline, speed and loop.
- Model facts, scene tree with bones, materials and textures, with missing texture files listed by name. Textures load from the model folder and its subfolders, TGA included.
- FBX files older than 7.0 get a note with their version and how to export them again.
- Zovo Pro tools: Convert to GLB, glTF, OBJ or STL; Asset check report; Render export (1 free try each).
